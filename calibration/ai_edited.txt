Time management is a skill that can improve both academic and professional outcomes, honestly more than people think. If you prioritise tasks well you cut down on stress and get more done. Setting clear goals gives direction and helps you stay focused throughout the day. Take breaks too, because the mind needs to rest and recharge! Calendars and to-do lists help organise things and make sure deadlines get met. Bottom line: good time management habits are worth it. They pay off long-term, for success and overall wellbeing.
